About

From prompt design to production pipelines.

I'm an AI/ML engineer currently working as a Prompt Engineer at Ailernova, where I design and optimize prompts for large language models to power automated content, script, and story generation — and build the API workflows that turn those prompts into scalable systems.

Before that, I spent over a year as a Data Science Intern at PetPooja, working hands-on with computer vision, NLP, Transformers, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) — from face-recognition data pipelines to evaluating LLM-based models in production-style settings.

I hold an MSc in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning from Gujarat University, and I like building things end-to-end: data, model, and interface.
